Owner tools

Only shown to the owner wallet. Use these if anything goes wrong.

  1. Pause the houseStops new bets, feeds and fee sweeps. Open bets can still settle; payouts and claims keep working.
  2. Withdraw from the houseOnly while paused. Sends SOL to your wallet: the bankroll first, then unclaimed coin profit, then stakes of open bets. Leave the amount empty to take everything.
  3. Close the program and reclaim its depositPermanent. Returns the program’s storage deposit to your wallet. Only allowed once the house is empty.
